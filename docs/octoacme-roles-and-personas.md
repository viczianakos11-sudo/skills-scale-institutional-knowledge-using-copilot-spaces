# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- With Engineering Lead: collaborate on technical direction and architecture
- With QA/Test Lead: discuss testability and test coverage expectations
- With UX/Design Lead: clarify implementation approach and feasibility
- With Security Lead: address security requirements and controls

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- With Project Manager: align on timeline, risks, and resource constraints
- With Project Sponsor: discuss business priorities and strategic alignment
- With Engineering Lead: explore technical feasibility and trade-offs
- With UX/Design Lead: refine user needs and design direction
- With Data/Analytics Lead: define success metrics and measurement approach
- With Customer Support Lead: incorporate customer feedback into prioritization

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- With Project Sponsor: escalate high-impact decisions and blockers
- With Product Manager: align on priorities and scope changes
- With Engineering Lead: discuss sequencing and technical dependencies
- With QA/Test Lead: coordinate quality gates and release readiness
- With Release/DevOps Lead: schedule deployments and monitor post-release
- With Security Lead: track security review timelines and remediation
- With all teams: facilitate blocker resolution and cross-team coordination

---

## Project Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors own executive alignment and strategic governance. They confirm business priority and funding, approve major scope or timeline changes, and resolve high-impact escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic priority, business value, and funding allocation
- Approve major changes to scope, timeline, or resource commitments
- Resolve escalations that impact business or organizational priorities
- Ensure alignment with company strategy and other initiatives
- Champion the project and remove organizational barriers

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers strategic business value
- Maintain executive visibility and stakeholder alignment
- Enable timely decision-making at governance level

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based executive briefings
- Escalation and decision approval channels
- Quarterly business reviews

### Key Interactions
- With Project Manager: for governance, escalations, and approval gates
- With Product Manager: for business outcomes and strategic priority discussions
- With stakeholders: communicate decisions and expectations

---

## Engineering Lead / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads own technical direction, architecture, and feasibility assessment. They lead design decisions, technical estimation, risk identification, and quality assurance at the architecture level.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design approach
- Make or guide major technical decisions and trade-offs
- Assess technical feasibility and estimation accuracy
- Identify technical dependencies and integration risks
- Mentor and support Developers; review technical implementations
- Collaborate on security, performance, and scalability requirements

### Goals
- Enable sustainable, scalable technical delivery
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Support team capability and knowledge transfer

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture sessions
- Planning and estimation discussions
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning

### Key Interactions
- With Product Manager: explore feasibility and trade-offs early
- With Project Manager: identify sequencing constraints and dependencies
- With Developers: guide design decisions and code quality
- With QA/Test Lead: discuss testability and quality approaches
- With Security Lead: incorporate security and performance requirements
- With Release/DevOps Lead: coordinate deployment and operability concerns

---

## UX/UI or Design Lead

### Role Summary
Design Leads own user research, interaction design, and visual design. They ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs and product vision.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user needs and personas
- Design interactions, workflows, and information architecture
- Create and maintain design systems and style guides
- Define accessibility requirements and validate compliance
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria and design validation
- Ensure visual consistency and brand alignment

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce support burden through clear design
- Validate assumptions through user testing

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specs and component documentation

### Key Interactions
- With Product Manager: refine user needs and prioritize features
- With Developers: discuss feasibility and implementation approach
- With QA/Test Lead: coordinate usability validation testing
- With Customer Support Lead: incorporate user feedback into improvements

---

## QA/Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Test Leads define quality standards, test strategy, and acceptance criteria. They assess release readiness and coordinate quality validation across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and quality acceptance criteria
- Establish test environments and test data requirements
- Identify quality risks and create mitigation plans
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Assess release readiness and document known issues
- Track defects and drive resolution

### Goals
- Ensure product quality meets customer and business expectations
- Reduce post-release incidents and support burden
- Enable confident, rapid release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and coverage documentation
- Quality metrics and risk assessment reports
- Release readiness assessments

### Key Interactions
- With Developers: discuss testability requirements and test coverage
- With Product Manager: clarify acceptance criteria and customer expectations
- With UX/Design Lead: plan usability and accessibility testing
- With Project Manager: coordinate quality gates and release approval
- With Release/DevOps Lead: prepare smoke tests and post-release verification
- With Engineering Lead: assess technical testing approach

---

## Security, Privacy, or Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads identify regulatory, privacy, threat, and security requirements. They coordinate reviews, track remediation, and ensure the project meets organizational and external compliance obligations.

### Responsibilities
- Identify regulatory, privacy, and security requirements
- Conduct or coordinate security reviews and threat assessments
- Define security controls and validation approaches
- Track security and compliance issues and remediation timelines
- Advise on trade-offs between security, performance, and usability
- Manage exceptions and document risk acceptance

### Goals
- Prevent security incidents and compliance violations
- Enable secure, compliant product delivery
- Maintain organizational and customer trust

### Typical Communication
- Security and compliance review checklists
- Risk assessment and remediation tracking
- Escalation and exception documentation

### Key Interactions
- With Project Manager: communicate security timeline and escalations
- With Developers and Engineering Lead: define controls and validation approach
- With Product Manager: discuss acceptable risk trade-offs
- With Release/DevOps Lead: ensure security controls in deployment

---

## Data/Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data/Analytics Leads define instrumentation, data quality, and measurement approaches. They enable evidence-based decision-making through dashboards and success metrics tracking.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and measurement approach
- Establish instrumentation and data collection requirements
- Design dashboards and reporting for key business and operational metrics
- Ensure data quality and accuracy
- Provide insights and analysis to inform decisions

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making
- Measure and demonstrate project impact
- Support continuous improvement through insights

### Typical Communication
- Dashboard and metric specifications
- Data collection and instrumentation documentation
- Insights and analysis reports

### Key Interactions
- With Product Manager: define success metrics and outcome measurement
- With Developers: coordinate instrumentation and telemetry implementation
- With Project Manager: provide progress dashboards and metrics
- With Release/DevOps Lead: track operational and performance metrics

---

## Release/DevOps or Site Reliability Engineer

### Role Summary
Release and DevOps Leads own deployment readiness, operational concerns, and post-release verification. They ensure reliable, observable deployments and rapid incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure deployment pipeline, infrastructure, and environments are ready
- Review deployment plans and rollback procedures
- Define observability, monitoring, and alerting requirements
- Coordinate deployments and post-release verification
- Support incident response and rollback if needed
- Capture operational insights and feed into continuous improvement

### Goals
- Enable safe, rapid deployments to production
- Minimize downtime and post-release incidents
- Support operational visibility and incident response

### Typical Communication
- Deployment checklists and runbooks
- Infrastructure and environment documentation
- Operational metrics and incident reports

### Key Interactions
- With Project Manager: schedule and coordinate deployments
- With QA/Test Lead: prepare and execute smoke tests
- With Engineering Lead: align on architecture and deployment approach
- With Developers: coordinate deployment and operational issues
- With Security Lead: implement security controls in deployment
- With Data/Analytics Lead: ensure operational metrics collection

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives bring customer-facing and operational insight. They prepare support readiness and feed customer feedback into product improvement decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare support documentation and runbooks
- Coordinate customer communication and release notes
- Identify support risks and prepare mitigation
- Feed recurring customer issues and requests into prioritization
- Participate in customer acceptance testing
- Support post-release customer communication

### Goals
- Ensure smooth customer experience during and after release
- Reduce support burden through clear documentation
- Capture customer insights for product improvement

### Typical Communication
- Support readiness checklists and documentation
- Customer feedback and issue summaries
- Release communication and customer briefings

### Key Interactions
- With Product Manager: provide customer feedback and prioritization input
- With Project Manager: communicate customer impact and concerns
- With Release/DevOps Lead: coordinate customer communication and timing
- With UX/Design Lead: share user experience feedback
- With QA/Test Lead: participate in customer acceptance testing

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Reference specific personas and interaction patterns when designing project workflows or escalation procedures.
- Adapt the personas and interactions to match your organization's structure while maintaining clear accountability and collaboration boundaries.
